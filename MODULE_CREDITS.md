# Module credits

In-game credit for each QoL module is the `author` field in
`OneWoW_QoL/Modules/external/<id>/module.lua` (the Details dialog). If that
field is omitted or empty, credit and copyright are the OneWoW Development
Team.

This file lists **community-authored** modules only. The named author keeps
copyright in that folder and licenses it to the OneWoW Development Team to
ship and maintain as part of official OneWoW (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)).
Do not remove their credit.

Team-written modules may still show a team member's name in Details. That is
credit, not a separate product, unless the module is listed here.
