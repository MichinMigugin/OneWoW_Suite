# OneWoW - Data: Quest Archive

**Classic through Dragonflight quest shards for the OneWoW Catalog.** The current expansion and the previous one stay in **OneWoW_CatalogData_Quests**. This hub plus its era packs load when you browse older expansions, search all quests, or look up quest rewards.

Each era is its own LoadOnDemand addon so picking Classic does not parse every older expansion at once:

- Classic through Burning Crusade
- Wrath of the Lich King through Cataclysm
- Mists of Pandaria through Warlords of Draenor
- Legion through Battle for Azeroth
- Shadowlands through Dragonflight

Players use it through Catalog. There is no standalone UI and no SavedVariables.

## Required Addons

- **OneWoW** - Core hub (required)
- **OneWoW_CatalogData_Quests** - Hot quest pack (loaded first when Archive is needed)
