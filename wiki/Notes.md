**OneWoW Notes** is a searchable notebook for players, NPCs, zones, items, collectibles, and free-form journals — with pins, hyperlinks, and account- or character-scoped storage.

**Requires:** [OneWoW](Home) core. Enable **Notes** under [Manage Features](Getting-Started).

**Open:** `/1wn` · `/own` · `/onewownotes` (see [Slash commands](Slash-Commands))

---

## Tabs (overview)

| Tab | Use it for |
|-----|------------|
| **Notes** | General journal — categories, tags, todos, favorites, login notes, pin-to-screen, rich text |
| **Players** | Guildmates and friends — realm, guild, class, personal comments; collectible sightings |
| **NPCs** | Vendors and important NPCs — location, stock, quests |
| **Zones** | Map pins and zone tips — quest hubs, rares, farming spots |
| **Items** | Item sources, farming routes, rewards — link items and search notes about them |
| **Collectibles** | Want / spotted / farming lists for mounts, pets, toys, transmog, and more |

---

## Collectibles

Track collectibles you care about with an **intent** (Want, Spotted, Farming, or Delete List):

* Filter by category, type, storage, and collected vs uncollected
* Live ownership comes from OneWoW’s collectibles service
* **Vendor capture** (Notes settings): Off, Prompt, or Automatic — add uncollected vendor collectibles to your want list when you browse merchants
* **Sold by** shows captured vendor offers (richer with Catalog Vendors data)
* Transmog sets can show ensemble progress and member appearances
* Optional **auto-delete**: collected Want/Spotted/Farming entries move to the Delete List, then purge after a delay (Immediate / 1 / 7 / 14 days); empty the Delete List anytime from settings

Link collectibles into journal notes the same way you link items.

---

## Handy features

* Hyperlinks to items, spells, NPCs, quests, achievements, and collectibles; cross-links between notes
* Coordinates and waypoints you can navigate to
* Account-wide or per-character storage
* Right-click helpers (target info, timestamps, character name)
* Auto-save as you type
* Suite themes and all eleven locales via OneWoW

QoL **Inspect Gear** can save inspect notes into Notes when that module is enabled.

---

## Tips

* Use account storage for guild/raid knowledge you want on every alt; character storage for private alts-only notes.
* Pin a short checklist note while doing a long questline.
* Turn on vendor collectible capture (Prompt or Automatic) when shopping for mounts/toys you still need.
